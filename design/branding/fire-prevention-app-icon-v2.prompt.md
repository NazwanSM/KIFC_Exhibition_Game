- Aset: [fire-prevention-app-icon-v2.png](fire-prevention-app-icon-v2.png).

- Arah visual: wajah maskot dan topi ranger sebagai fokus, seragam merah, hutan hijau, pencahayaan amber, serta aksen api/daun.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: logo-brand / identity-preserve.
Create ONE finished square app icon for the existing forest-fire-prevention educational game, using the attached project mascot as the essential identity reference. The previous abstract shield icon felt too plain; this should feel like an inviting, polished game icon with the recognizable mascot as the star.
Preserve the reference orangutan's exact identity: warm orange face and reddish fur, large friendly eyes, gentle smile, distinctive black broad-brim ranger hat with yellow band, red Manggala Agni ranger uniform with reflective yellow/silver details and dark straps. Keep the existing left-chest patch if visible, do not invent different branding.
Composition: close-up chest-up portrait, large expressive face, slight welcoming three-quarter turn while looking at viewer. Hat and face are the dominant silhouette; shoulders and a little red uniform visible below. No full body or small distant character. Keep hat tips and head within a central adaptive-mask-safe circle; uncluttered outer margin.
Background: rich deep forest green #0C2B1F / #1F4A39 with a restrained softly lit canopy silhouette, a broad warm amber halo shape behind the ranger for clear contrast. Add ONE compact amber/orange flame-and-leaf badge near the lower-right shoulder, subordinate to the face, communicating protecting the forest from fire. Use the reference's clean illustrated shading and confident outlines, slightly simplify fine textures for small-icon legibility. High-quality warm game artwork, not flat corporate clipart or glossy plastic 3D.
Square 1:1 full-bleed canvas, sharp high-resolution illustration. No words, initials, slogans, numbers, additional partner marks, watermarks, devices, mockups, multiple variants, busy forest scenery or outer rounded-corner border. Do not change the mascot species, face, hat or uniform.
```
