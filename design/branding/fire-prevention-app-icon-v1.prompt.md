## Prompt final

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: app launcher icon / application logo for "Fire Prevention Challenge", an educational touchscreen game about forest fire prevention for the Korea–Indonesia Forest Center exhibition.
Create ONE finished square app icon, not a presentation board or multiple variations.
Design a distinctive, highly legible single integrated emblem: a broad ivory protective shield, with an elegant forest-green tree/leaf motif embracing a small warm amber flame in its central negative space. The tree/leaf and flame should feel like one intelligently constructed mark rather than separate clipart. Emphasize protection of forests and understanding fire, welcoming and educational rather than emergency alarm. Generous rounded organic silhouettes, bold geometry, a balanced mature game identity. Clear recognizability at 48 pixels.
Color palette from the existing app: deep forest green #0C2B1F and #1F4A39, ivory #F7F5EE, warm amber #E9A814; restrained tiny orange accent only if needed within the flame. Solid deep forest green background extending to all four canvas edges. An extremely subtle tonal green variation is acceptable, keep the emblem essentially flat.
Composition: square 1:1, centered large emblem occupying about 65 percent of canvas width and height; comfortable 17 percent safe margins so Android adaptive masks do not cut the emblem. Export-looking high resolution crisp raster with vector-clean contours, professional deliberate negative space, no visual clutter.
No lettering, initials, numbers, slogans, partner logos, watermarks, icon grids, UI, devices, mockup shadows, photographic textures, neon, metal, bevels, 3D extrusion, gradients across the emblem, tiny foliage details, warning triangle, fire extinguisher, or cute faces. Do not pre-round or frame the outer canvas. Produce the actual full-bleed icon asset.
```
